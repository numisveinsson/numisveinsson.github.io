Gave a keynote talk on **“Scalable Automation of Cardiovascular Geometric Modeling for Patient-Specific Simulation”** at [WCCM-ECCOMAS 2026](https://wccm-eccomas2026.org/) in Munich, Germany. Presented in mini-symposium **MS120 — Recent Advances in Computational Methods for the Cardiovascular System**. The talk covered **MeshGrow**, **LinFlo-Net**, and **SeqSeg**, citing [MeshGrow (JRSM Cardiovascular Disease, 2026)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/20480040261455944), the earlier [MeshGrow conference paper (FIMH 2025)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-94562-5_10), [LinFlo-Net (Journal of Biomechanical Engineering, 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4064527), and [SeqSeg (Annals of Biomedical Engineering, 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10439-024-03611-z).
