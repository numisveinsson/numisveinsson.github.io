Research Experience

Focus on computational modeling, artificial intelligence, and digital twin development for cardiovascular and respiratory health. I develop data-driven frameworks that integrate deep learning with physics-based modeling to automate anatomical reconstruction, simulation, and translational clinical tools. My work bridges biomedical engineering, scientific computing, and AI to advance precision medicine.

Datasets and Tools

  • SeqSeg — Deep learning–based framework for automated vascular segmentation and model construction from CT and MR images. Includes preprocessing pipeline, vessel segment extraction method, and publicly released pretrained model weights. github.com/numisveinsson/SeqSeg
  • MeshGrow — Integrated framework for unified cardiac and vascular mesh construction from medical images, enabling automatic generation of anatomically consistent, simulation-ready 3D cardiovascular models. github.com/numisveinsson/MeshGrow
  • LinFlo-Net — Deep learning framework for cardiac segmentation and modeling. github.com/ArjunNarayanan/LinFlo-Net
  • SimVascular — Open-source cardiovascular modeling and simulation software. Contributed to feature development and testing in collaboration with the Marsden Lab at Stanford University. simvascular.github.io
  • MIROS (Medical Image to Reduced Order Simulation) — Toolkit for linking patient-specific medical imaging data to reduced-order hemodynamic simulations, facilitating efficient cardiovascular modeling and parameter estimation. github.com/BryannGan/MIROS
  • Vascular Model Repository — Collaborative online platform for sharing, visualizing, and comparing vascular geometries and simulation data; developed in partnership with the Marsden Lab to promote open science in cardiovascular modeling. vascularmodel.com
  • SlicerSeqSeg — 3D Slicer extension for automated vascular segmentation using SeqSeg. github.com/numisveinsson/SlicerSeqSeg
  • SlicerLinFloNet — 3D Slicer extension for vascular segmentation and modeling with LinFlo-Net. github.com/numisveinsson/SlicerLinFloNet
